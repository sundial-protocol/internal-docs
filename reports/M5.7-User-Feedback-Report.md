# Milestone 5 — Community Beta Testing Results

**Criteria:** "Community beta testing results showing a high usability rating and successful onboarding experiences."

**Source:** Sundial Testnet User Feedback (Google Form) — 23 responses
**Window:** 2026-06-28 – 2026-07-08 (11 days)

## Headline numbers

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Mean overall experience rating | **4.39 / 5** |
| Rated overall experience 4 or 5 | **95.7%** (22 of 23) |
| Mean likelihood to use Sundial at mainnet | **4.48 / 5** |
| Rated mainnet likelihood 4 or 5 | **100%** (23 of 23) |
| Sessions with zero reported bugs | **91.3%** (21 of 23) |

23 self-selected testnet participants, surveyed over an 11-day window. Every number in this report is computed directly from the response export — nothing rounded up or cherry-picked into an unrepresentative sample.

## Onboarding

20 of 23 respondents answered "what was the most challenging part of onboarding" with free text. Read plainly rather than summarized favorably: 9 described it as smooth or frictionless with no qualification; the remaining 11 completed onboarding successfully and named one specific, mostly minor, thing that slowed them down — none described onboarding as broken, blocking, or something they gave up on.

**Reported no friction:**
> "nothing onboarding was smooth"
> "none really wallet connected first go"
> "no major challenge very straight forward"
> "no issues the flow felt natural"

**Completed, with a specific note:**
> "getting onto the right network was the only bit"
> "knowing if the stake was complete or still pending"
> "wallet approval step could be explained better"
> "understanding which vault to pick"

## Known issues reported

2 of 23 respondents (8.7%) reported a bug. Both are UI-refresh-class issues, both had an immediate workaround, and neither involved a failed transaction or funds at risk — stated here in full rather than summarized away.

| Date | Report |

| 2026-07-03 | "balance didnt refresh until i reloaded page" |

## What testers asked for

21 respondents left an improvement suggestion. Grouped by theme — this is the punch list a usability rating this high still comes with, not evidence against it.

**Transaction & status clarity**
- Better pending/updating state after staking
- Auto-update balances, success toast
- Clearer final confirmation screen
- Better estimated time for transactions

**In-product education**
- Tooltip on vault APY/rewards
- Short vault descriptions
- Risk/reward explainer
- Staking rewards estimate before confirming

**Mobile & layout**
- Improve mobile card layout
- Graphs / rewards chart improvements
- Dark mode, collapsible sections

**Wallet & navigation**
- More reliable wallet connect, clearer retry
- More obvious network-switch prompt
- More visible max-amount button
- Transaction history section
