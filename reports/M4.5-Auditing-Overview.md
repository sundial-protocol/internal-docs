## Reviewers

### Hacken

Hacken is a leading blockchain security firm known for smart contract audits, penetration testing, and dApp security assessments. Founded in 2017, they have conducted hundreds of audits across major blockchain ecosystems and are widely recognized in the industry for their rigorous methodology and public audit reports.

They have audited prominent projects such as Binance, OKX, Bybit, Gate.io, NEAR, Polygon, Solana, Arbitrum, 1inch, MetaMask, and Crypto.com.

https://hacken.io

We chose Hacken for our staking and DeFi audits due to their top-tier expertise with complex smart contract systems and their reputation for thorough, actionable audit reports.

### Dual Defense (By Hackenproof)

Dual Defense is an audit program run by Hackenproof — Hacken's separate bug bounty and crowdsourced security platform. While Hacken provides traditional point-in-time security audits, Hackenproof operates independently as a continuous security platform, coordinating competing security researchers to stress-test a target over an extended period. Dual Defense combines both approaches: a structured audit followed by an open bug bounty campaign.

Dual Defense has been used by projects such as OKX, Somnia, and Bluefin to further harden their security posture after an initial audit.

https://hackenproof.com

We chose Dual Defense for the same purpose. After the professional audit, we wanted to further stress-test our system with a wider range of security researchers and real-world attack scenarios, which is exactly what Dual Defense provides.
